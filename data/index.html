Before you start

Short silent clips, one yes or no question each. Everything stays on this laptop.

  • Watch the whole clip, then answer the question about that clip only.
  • Click the clip to pause or resume it; click once it has finished to replay. Replay as often as you like. R replays, Y is yes, N is no.
  • Some clips look very similar to each other. That is on purpose. Judge each one on its own.
  • The clips are silent, because the models we compare against only see the frames.
  • You can go back over anything you have already answered to check it, and next forward again without re-answering. Arrow keys work too.
  • Your progress is saved after every answer, so you can close the tab and come back.
Use the same name if you come back later; that is how your progress is restored.
